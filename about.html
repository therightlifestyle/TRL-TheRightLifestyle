About

TRL builds AI systems for businesses. That's it.

We are not a marketing agency that added "AI" to its homepage. We are not a SaaS company selling you another subscription. We are a founder-led AI automation company based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, building custom systems for businesses worldwide.

Mission

Remove the repetitive work that keeps business owners from the work that matters.

Most businesses don't have an AI problem. They have a repetition problem — the same replies, the same data entry, the same follow-ups, the same reports, every single day. That work eats the founder's time, caps growth and makes running the business feel like treading water. We build systems that do that work — so the humans can do the work only humans can do.

Vision

A world where small and mid-sized businesses can access the same operational leverage as enterprise companies — without enterprise price tags.

AI and automation shouldn't be reserved for companies with 50-person ops teams and seven-figure software budgets. We build systems that a 3-person business can deploy in a week, run themselves, and actually see a return from.

Promise

We tell you when you don't need us.

If your business isn't ready for automation, if a $50 tool solves it, if the ROI isn't there yet — we will tell you. The $35 Workflow Audit exists partly so we can say "don't spend more" when that is the right answer. Honest advice costs us short-term sales and buys us long-term trust.

Values

Four values that shape every decision.

Honesty over sales

We will tell you not to buy from us when that is the right answer. We will tell you the price before we ask for a payment. We will tell you when something is hard, in development, or outside our skill set. Sales without honesty is just lying with a nicer outfit.

Ship real things

We don't sell strategies that stay on slides or audits that gather dust. Every engagement ends with a working system, written documentation and a trained team — or a clear, honest reason why it shouldn't be built yet.

Stay small, stay accountable

TRL intentionally stays founder-led. That means the person selling is the person building is the person on WhatsApp at 9pm fixing a thing you flagged. Scale happens through systems and leverage — not by adding layers between the client and the work.

Build for the long term

A system that breaks when Rashid is asleep is a liability, not leverage. Everything we ship is documented, handed over and designed for your team to run. We don't build dependence on us — we build independence for you.

Proof-first honesty

Here is the line we don't cross.

✕

What we will never claim

  • Fabricated revenue lifts, time-saved figures or client results
  • Fake testimonials, ratings or invented client logos
  • "10x your business" or other garbage promises
  • "Buy" buttons for systems we haven't built yet
  • Team members who don't exist
✓

What we prove instead

  • Real founder, real phone number, real WhatsApp
  • Real answers when "not yet" is the right answer
  • Real systems, documented, handed over, working
  • Building in public — including unfinished parts
  • Every quote confirmed in writing before work starts
