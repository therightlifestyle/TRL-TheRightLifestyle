Privacy

What this site collects: almost nothing.

TRL would rather build AI systems than run a surveillance network. Here is exactly what happens when you visit this website — and what doesn't.

What lives in your browser only

This site stores exactly two values in your browser's localStorage:

  • trl-theme — your dark/light theme preference
  • trl-currency — whether prices default to USD or PKR

These never leave your device, contain no personal data and are used only to remember your preference on your next visit.

What this site does NOT do

  • No Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Hotjar or any tracking script
  • No cookies — not even "essential" ones
  • No ad networks, retargeting or fingerprinting
  • No login, no accounts, no sign-ups
  • No form submissions to our servers (the brief builder runs entirely in your browser and only sends when you press WhatsApp)
  • No AI chatbot on this site that records your conversations

Third parties loaded on this site

Three external services are used. Two are font/CDN providers that see your IP as part of normal browser operation; one is WhatsApp for contact links.

Google Fonts

Loads typography (Sora, Instrument Sans, JetBrains Mono) from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives your IP and browser user-agent as part of the font request per normal HTTP.

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs standard HTTP request data (IP, user-agent, URL requested) to serve the site and prevent abuse. That data is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

WhatsApp (on click)

WhatsApp links only load wa.me when you click them. If you message TRL on WhatsApp, the conversation is governed by WhatsApp's terms and TRL's own confidentiality practices — we never share client messages.

When you contact TRL

When you message us on WhatsApp or email officialtrlservice@gmail.com, we receive whatever you choose to send us (your name, business details, project description). We use that information only to reply to you and scope the work you're asking about. We don't sell client information, we don't add you to any mailing list without asking, and we delete briefs when they're no longer needed.

Questions?

Message us on WhatsApp or email officialtrlservice@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions the same way we answer pricing questions — honestly and like a human wrote it.
