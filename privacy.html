Privacy

Privacy by architecture, not by policy theatre.

No analytics SDKs, no ad trackers, no marketing cookies, no profiles, no resale. This page is short because there is genuinely very little to disclose.

What we collect

  • Your message — only if you choose to send one on WhatsApp or email.
  • Your email address — only if you write to us, so we can reply.
  • Project details — the files, access and context needed to scope and deliver work you asked for.

That's the list. There is no tracking pixel, no session recording and no third-party analytics script anywhere on this site.

What stays on your device

  • Theme preference — stored in your browser's local storage (trl-theme) so the site remembers dark or light mode.
  • Currency emphasis — stored locally (trl-currency) so the USD/PKR switch stays where you left it.
  • The brief builder — every word you type stays in your browser until you press send. Nothing is transmitted, logged or saved anywhere else.

Both local values are non-identifying preferences. Clearing your browser storage removes them instantly.

Third parties we do use

  • Google Fonts — typography is loaded from Google's CDN, which means your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads. No cookies are set by this site as a result.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only when you click a WhatsApp link or button. At that point Meta's own privacy terms apply to the conversation.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes standard server requests (IP address, user agent) to serve the page.

We tell you about these three because pretending otherwise would violate the honesty standard this whole brand is built on.

Data retention

Project details are kept for the duration of delivery plus 30 days for revisions and reporting, then deleted. WhatsApp and email conversations are kept for reference unless you ask us to delete them — and if you ask, we delete them, no negotiation.

Your controls

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete a conversation or project file.
  • Ask us not to keep deliverables after handover.

Email rashidmuhammadamir@gmail.com or message WhatsApp. Requests are handled by the founder, not a support queue.

Security, honestly

We use strong passwords, two-factor authentication on our accounts, and least-privilege access to client tools. We do not claim certifications we don't hold. If a project requires a security review or an NDA, say so in the brief and we'll sign it before you share anything sensitive.
