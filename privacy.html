Privacy

Privacy by architecture

We collect only what we need to deliver. No analytics SDKs. No ad trackers. No cookies. No third-party UI libraries. Local tools (TRL Focus, Lead OS) never upload anything.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp message (only if you initiate it).
  • Your email (only if you write to us).
  • Project details (only to scope work before it starts).

What we don't collect

  • No browsing tracking.
  • No cookies.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • No data uploads from local tools.

Data retention

Project details are kept for the duration of delivery plus 30 days for revisions and reporting, then deleted. WhatsApp and email conversations are kept for reference unless you ask us to delete them.
