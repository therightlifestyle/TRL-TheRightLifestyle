Pricing questions, answered

Core tiers ($35, $299, $799, $1,999) are fixed prices for the scope described. Custom scopes, extra integrations or unusual deadlines may change the number — and that number is always confirmed in writing before any work begins. No checkout button exists on this site on purpose.

Core packages have fixed PKR prices because they're well-defined. For custom scopes and add-ons, PKR pricing is locked when we confirm the quote on WhatsApp — exchange rates move and we don't pretend otherwise.

The TRL Standard applies: miss the written delivery date → 100% refund. A near-miss → 10% back plus a priority rush. The $35 Workflow Audit refunds automatically if it lands after day five, and you keep the deliverables.
