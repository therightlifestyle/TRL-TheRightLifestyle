04 · Lead & Customer Response

Stop losing leads because nobody replied fast enough.

Most businesses lose more leads to slow response than to bad pricing. A proper response system means every enquiry gets a reply within 60 seconds, qualified while you sleep, and handed to you warm.

What a typical response system does

  • Captures leads from WhatsApp, Instagram DM, website forms and email in one place
  • Replies within 60 seconds with on-brand, business-trained messaging
  • Qualifies against your criteria (budget, timeline, service fit)
  • Books calls or routes hot leads directly to you
  • Sends automatic follow-ups for 3–14 days if there's no reply
  • Logs everything in a CRM or dashboard you can actually read
  • Hands off to a human the moment a human is needed

Why "just hire someone to reply" isn't the answer

  • People sleep, take weekends and leave jobs
  • Response time in the first 5 minutes is 21× more likely to qualify
  • A new hire takes weeks to train on your business; an agent takes days
  • Manual replies are inconsistent in tone, accuracy and follow-through
  • The agent doesn't replace your team — it handles the 80% repeat questions so they handle the 20% that close deals
