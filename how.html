Process

From first message to a system that runs on its own.

Five steps, one accountable founder, everything written down. No sales calls disguised as discovery. No vague proposals. No handoff to a junior you've never met.

5 steps3–20 days end-to-endWritten scope before paymentOn-time guarantee
01

Audit — see what's actually happening

We start every engagement with the $35 Workflow Audit. You tell us how the business runs today, where leads come from, what repeats every day, where you spend hours you shouldn't. We map it out in writing.

You get five deliverables: the leaks we found, ranked by cost; the repetitive steps that should be automated; what to build first; what to not build yet; and honest answers to "is this worth it".

Day 0–5 · $35 · refunded if late past day 5
02

Scope — written proposal, fixed price, fixed timeline

Based on the audit you get a written proposal. Exactly what we'll build, which tools connect, how the AI will be trained, what we need from you, fixed price, fixed delivery date, and what happens after launch.

You ask questions, we revise until it's clear. Nothing starts until you confirm the scope in writing. No invoice appears that wasn't in the proposal.

Day 5–6 · free · no obligation
03

Build — the system gets built around your real data

Rashid builds the system: agents, automations, integrations, dashboards. We use your real customer messages, your real pricing, your real tone of voice — not generic templates.

You get access to the build as it progresses. No black-box "we'll show you when it's done". You review agent replies, test workflows and flag edge cases before anything goes near a real customer.

Day 6–20 · build window (depends on tier)
04

Test & launch — human-reviewed before go-live

We test against real scenarios: common questions, edge cases, rude customers, off-topic messages, broken inputs. We fix what fails. You sign off on how the AI sounds, how fast replies land and what routes to a human.

Then we flip it on. We monitor the first 48 hours closely and fix anything that doesn't behave right in production — at our cost.

Within agreed ETA · human sign-off required
05

Handover & improve — you own the system

You get documentation: how the system works, how to update the AI's knowledge, what to do if something breaks, checklists for your team. On Growth OS and Premium Scale we include a live walkthrough.

Premium clients get 30 days of support and iteration included. After that, ongoing optimisation is optional — we monitor, tune and expand as your business changes. Many clients stay on monthly retainers; many don't. Either way, the system is yours.

Ongoing · optional

How we work differently

A few things worth knowing up front.

We will tell you not to buy from us

If your business doesn't actually have a problem AI can solve, if you're too early, if the cost doesn't make sense versus the return — we'll tell you. The $35 audit exists partly so we can say "don't spend more" when that's the right answer. We'd rather lose a quick sale than build something you don't need.

We don't lock you in

Every system is built on tools you own or can access directly. You get full documentation, credentials where appropriate, and the ability to take over or move to another provider. No proprietary black boxes, no hostage situations, no monthly "platform fee" for something we built once.

No fabricated case studies

TRL is building in public. We don't invent client results, paste stock photos of fake "team members" or claim to have "100+ businesses transformed" when we haven't. What we've done is listed honestly; what we haven't done yet isn't claimed.

AI is a tool. Humans stay in charge.

We don't build systems that pretend to be you, lie about being human, or close deals without you. Our agents qualify, inform, route and handle the repeat work — and hand off to a real person the moment a real person is needed.
